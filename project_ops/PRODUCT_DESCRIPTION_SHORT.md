# Spayce — Short Product Description

## One-liner

Spayce is a desktop AI OS for knowledge workers — one app to browse, organize, learn, build, and delegate to AI, with every artifact saved in a single connected knowledge base.

---

## The Problem

Modern knowledge workers live across 8–12 apps simultaneously: Chrome with 30 tabs, Notion for notes, Obsidian for PKM, VS Code for code, Slack for comms, Google Drive for files, and a dozen AI chat windows. Context is fragmented. Nothing connects. The AI tools they use have zero memory of what they actually do all day.

The result: hours lost to app-switching, copy-pasting, and re-explaining context that should already be known.

---

## The Product

**Spayce** is a single desktop application — built on Electron — that replaces the entire scattered stack with one unified, AI-native environment.

At its core is a **hierarchical knowledge base**: every website you browse, note you write, file you open, meeting you record, and code artifact you create gets organized into a structured folder system inside Spayce. This becomes your permanent, searchable, ever-growing personal knowledge graph — your own context layer for AI.

On top of this:

- **Integrated Browser** — Browse the web inside the app. Pages you visit get captured into your knowledge base automatically. No more lost tabs.
- **AI Agent Workspace** — A built-in AI agent that knows everything in your knowledge base. Ask it to summarize a meeting transcript, draft a document from a set of notes, send an email, or search across all your stored context.
- **Notes & Documents** — Rich markdown notes, linked to any item in your knowledge base. Notion-like editing without the subscription.
- **STT & Meeting Logs** — Record calls directly in the app. Transcripts are auto-saved into the relevant project folder.
- **IDE & Terminal** — Write and run code inside the same workspace. The AI agent understands your code, your docs, and your browser history together.
- **Local File Browser** — Navigate your filesystem from within the app. Open, view, and organize local files as first-class items alongside web content.

---

## Who It's For

- **Indie founders & solo builders** who need to move fast without a team
- **Students & researchers** managing a growing knowledge base across sources
- **Knowledge workers** who want AI assistance grounded in their actual work
- **Enterprises** needing an auditable, sandboxed, monitored AI work environment
